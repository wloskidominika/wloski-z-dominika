What to see in Tropea: 7 places not to miss

Wondering what to see in Tropea when you only have a few days? The "Pearl of the Tyrrhenian" is small, but full of corners worth a stop: an old town perched on a cliff above the sea, pale beaches right below the houses and sunsets that look painted. Here are the seven stops we always recommend to our guests.

1. The old town, slowly

The best way to start is to get lost on foot. Between paved alleys, stone doorways and small squares you discover noble palaces, craft shops and balconies overlooking the sea. In the evening the main street fills with people, ice-cream shops and the smell of Calabrian cooking.

2. Santa Maria dell'Isola

This is the iconic image of Tropea: a sanctuary on top of a rock, reached by a flight of steps. From the top the view sweeps over the coast and the beaches, so keep your camera ready.

3. The Cathedral

In the heart of the town stands the Cathedral, of Norman origin. Even if museums are not your thing, a few minutes inside give you a moment of calm and cool air during the hottest hours.

4. The beaches below the cliff

One of the best things about Tropea is that the sea is a short walk from the centre: you go down on foot and find pale sand and clear water. If you fancy a change, there are more beautiful beaches along the coast.

5. Sunset from the viewpoint

When the sun goes down, from the viewpoints in the centre you can often see the outline of Stromboli on the horizon. Arrive a little early: the best spots fill up fast.

6. Food and the red onion

No visit is complete without tasting the red onion of Tropea, together with 'nduja, chilli and country bread. Many restaurants in the centre serve it in lots of different ways.

7. The coast from the sea

To really understand what to see in Tropea and the surrounding area, spend half a day on a boat: coves, caves and crystal-clear water all the way to Capo Vaticano. You will find all our boat trips from Tropea, including day trips to the Aeolian Islands.

Where to stay and how to get here

To enjoy Tropea at a relaxed pace, it is best to stay near the centre or around Capo Vaticano: take a look at our holiday homes. If you fly into Lamezia Terme, the private transfer takes you straight to your door.
