Capo Vaticano: beaches, coves and views

Capo Vaticano is the best-known headland on the Coast of the Gods, in the municipality of Ricadi, a few kilometres from Tropea. Sheer cliffs, pale sand and a sea in incredible colours make it a must for anyone spending a holiday in this part of Calabria.

The most beautiful beaches

The best known is Grotticelle beach, with clear water and a seabed that is perfect for snorkelling. Along the coast you will also find many small coves and inlets, some easy to reach on foot, others only from the sea. If you like a quiet sea, go early in the morning or towards evening.

The Capo Vaticano viewpoint

The Capo Vaticano viewpoint opens onto one of the widest panoramas of the Tyrrhenian Sea. On clear days you can see the Aeolian Islands and the outline of Stromboli. It is the perfect place to watch the sunset.

Capo Vaticano from the sea

The best way to discover the hidden coves is a boat trip from Tropea: you sail along the coast, enter the most beautiful bays and stop several times for a swim. You can choose a boat or dinghy tour, or rent a dinghy that needs no licence and decide for yourself where to stop.

When to go

For swimming, the ideal period is from June to September. Spring and early autumn are perfect if you prefer milder temperatures, fewer people and scenic walks.

Where to stay in Capo Vaticano

Staying here means having the sea a few minutes away and Tropea very close. Among our holiday homes you will find sea-view apartments and a villa with a panoramic pool among the olive trees. To get around between beaches and villages, take a look at our car and scooter rental.

In short

  • Beach not to miss: Grotticelle.
  • Best moment for the view: sunset from the viewpoint.
  • Recommended experience: a boat trip among the coves.
