Tropea red onion: how to enjoy it, with recipes

The Tropea red onion is one of the symbols of Calabria: sweet, crunchy and a bright purple colour you recognise instantly on the market stalls. Anyone who spends a few days on the Coast of the Gods finds it everywhere, from salads to jams. Here is how to enjoy it at its best.

Why it is so special

Compared with ordinary onions it has a delicate, mild flavour, so much so that many people eat it raw. The secret lies in the mild climate and the soil of the coast between Tropea and Capo Vaticano. That is why it has become famous throughout Italy and abroad.

Tropea salad

This is the simplest and best-loved way to taste it. You need:

  • Tropea red onion, thinly sliced;
  • fresh tomatoes;
  • olives;
  • oregano and extra virgin olive oil;
  • a pinch of salt.

Mix everything and leave it to rest for a few minutes. With a slice of country bread it becomes a perfect summer lunch.

Pasta with onions

The red onion is wonderful cooked, too. Slice it finely and let it soften over a low heat with olive oil until it becomes soft and almost sweet. You can add a few cherry tomatoes or let the onion be the star, then toss it with pasta, perhaps with a drizzle of raw olive oil.

Sweet and sour, and jam

In the shops of Tropea's old town you will also find it sweet and sour or as a jam, excellent with mature cheeses. It is a simple, tasty gift to take home.

Where to taste it

Almost every restaurant in the centre serves it in some form. For a more authentic experience, try a day in the countryside or a farmhouse dinner: you will find them among our experiences and excursions. And to discover the rest of the town, read our guide on what to see in Tropea.

One last tip: taste the Tropea red onion right here. Fresh from the field, it has a flavour that is hard to find back home. And if you are looking for somewhere to stay, take a look at our holiday homes.
