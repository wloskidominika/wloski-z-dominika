Beaches near Tropea and Capo Vaticano: where to go

The beaches near Tropea are one of the main reasons people choose the Coast of the Gods: pale sand, clear water and scenery that changes at every bend. Some are a few steps from the centre, others are hidden among the cliffs of Capo Vaticano. Here is a short guide to help you find your way.

The beaches below Tropea's old town

At the foot of the cliff where the old town stands, you can walk down in a few minutes. It is ideal if you don't want to take the car: swim, have lunch in town and go back to the sea in the afternoon. In July and August it is worth arriving early to find a spot.

Capo Vaticano and its coves

A few kilometres from Tropea, the Capo Vaticano headland offers coves, rocks and a sea that shades from green to deep blue. It is perfect for snorkelling and views: from the viewpoint, on clear days, you can see the Aeolian Islands. We have written a whole article about Capo Vaticano and its beaches.

Torre Ruffa and the Ricadi coast

Between Tropea and Capo Vaticano there are long stretches of sand, with equipped beach clubs and free areas. Many of our holiday homes are right in this area, a few minutes from Torre Ruffa beach.

Beaches you can only reach by sea

Some of the most beautiful coves have no road: you can only get there by boat. On a boat or dinghy tour you stop to swim where you can't get to from land. If you prefer to be independent, you can also rent a dinghy that needs no licence.

Practical tips

  • In the hottest months arrive early in the morning: car parks fill up quickly.
  • Bring water shoes for the rocky coves.
  • Don't forget water, a hat and sunscreen: some beaches have no facilities.
  • To move between beaches without parking problems, a rental scooter is very handy.
