Aeolian Islands from Tropea: how to plan the trip

Visiting the Aeolian Islands from Tropea is one of the highlights of a holiday in Calabria. On clear days you can already see them on the horizon from the town's viewpoint, and on a mini cruise you can visit them in a day, returning in the evening. Here is how to choose the right tour.

The islands to know

  • Stromboli: the ever-active volcano, famous for eruptions that you can see from the sea after dark.
  • Lipari: the largest island, with its castle, old town and many little streets to explore.
  • Vulcano: its thermal mud pools and the smell of sulphur make it unique.
  • Panarea: small and elegant, with white houses and crystal-clear bays.

Full day or sunset at Stromboli?

On the full-day trip you visit Vulcano, Lipari and Stromboli: departure from Tropea harbour is at 7:45 and you are back around 18:40. There is also a route to Panarea and Stromboli to see the Sciara del Fuoco at sunset, a natural spectacle that is hard to forget. You will find all the details on the excursions from Tropea page.

How much it costs

For the full-day mini cruise, prices start at €65 for adults and €45 for children aged 3 to 10, plus a €5 landing tax. For groups of up to 8 people you can also hire a private dinghy or boat.

When to go

The best period is from June to September, when the sea is calmer. Departures always depend on the weather: book a few days ahead and keep a back-up date.

What to bring

  • Swimsuit, towel and comfortable shoes for walking on the islands.
  • Sunscreen, a hat and water.
  • A light sweatshirt for the evening return.
  • Some cash for small expenses on the islands.

Before and after the trip

The boat leaves early in the morning, so staying close to the harbour makes everything easier. Have a look at our holiday homes in Tropea. And if you are flying in, book the airport transfer to start your holiday stress-free.
